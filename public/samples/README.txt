SentrAID sample photos
======================
Put your own Quezon City photos here, using EXACTLY these file names (JPG):

  accident.jpg
  fire-smoke.jpg
  road-damage.jpg
  road-obstruction.jpg
  infrastructure-damage.jpg
  flooding.jpg
  structural-damage.jpg
  normal.jpg

If a file is missing, the app shows a stock placeholder photo for that class.
Tip: use photos you took yourselves, or CC-licensed ones from Wikimedia Commons
(search "Quezon City" there) and credit them in your documentation.
